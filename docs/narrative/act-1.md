# Act 1 — Debt (Tamori)

## Emotional Core

The player loses something before they understand its value. Then they're in motion — not because they chose to be, but because someone owes them.

This act is about establishing: what the world is, who these two people are, and what the Veil actually does to ordinary life. Everything is gentle until it isn't.

---

## Opening Sequence

**Cold open.** A wheat field at dusk. No music. The air shimmers — barely visible, a heat-haze that doesn't move right. A woman walks through the field carrying a basket. She doesn't see the shimmer. It becomes a gash. She steps too close. She's gone. The basket falls. The field is empty.

*Silence. Then the title: WAYFARER.*

---

**Morning. Tamori.**

The player wakes up as Liris. Not a tutorial prompt — just her, in her house, her morning. Feed the chickens. Check on the garden. The camera is slow. Cherry blossoms. Mist. A neighbor calling across the fence. This takes five minutes and that's deliberate. The player is meant to feel like this place is real and worth being in.

Distant sounds: shouting, breaking wood, something crashing. Getting closer.

Sarro comes through the rice field at a jog, trailed by two men he clearly just fought in the village tavern. He's not scared — he's mildly irritated, the way you are when something goes slightly wrong. He clips the post holding her barn lantern on the way through. He doesn't notice. The lantern falls.

The fire is slow at first.

Sarro dispatches the two men (first combat — short, instructive, shows his style), turns around, sees the barn. Looks at the lantern post. Looks at Liris.

Then: the air next to the burning barn *cracks*. A Veil-tear, small and violent, opening in the heat and disruption. Sarro's proximity to it — divine blood, chaotic transit energy — is enough. Liris's person (the one she loves, established in a brief earlier scene or just implied by her calling out their name) steps out of the barn to see what's happening. Stands too close to the tear.

Gone.

Liris looks at the empty space. Looks at Sarro. He knows what happened. He doesn't have words for it.

**This is the debt.**

---

## Tamori — Scene by Scene

### Scene 1: The Village is Wrong

After the immediate disaster, Liris refuses to go through the tear after her person (it's unstable, Sarro physically prevents her — the only time he overrides her in the whole game). He pledges to get her through safely, to find whoever she lost, to fix what he broke. She doesn't accept this warmly. She accepts it practically.

The village has its own problem: a larger tear opened in the eastern rice fields three weeks ago and hasn't closed. The crops are failing. People are frightened. A delegation asks for help — Liris because she's known, Sarro because he's a stranger with a sword and strangers sometimes help.

*First open-world moment:* Tamori is small but dense. The player can talk to everyone. There are three or four minor side moments — a family argument about whether to leave, a child who saw something come through the tear, an old man who remembers the last Veil event fifty years ago and isn't frightened ("they always close").

### Scene 2: The Eastern Fields

The tear in the rice fields is older and stranger than the one that took Liris's person. Something came through it — not a creature exactly, more like a *condition*. The water in the flooded paddies is wrong. Crops die in it. Animals won't go near it.

Combat here is optional and strange. What came through is confused and lost, not hostile. The player can fight it or figure out what it is and help it find its way back. Either ends the immediate problem; the latter gives more information about how tears work.

**Meeting Idris:** She's been camped at the edge of the eastern fields for two weeks. Veil Warden — old order, mostly disbanded, still doing the work alone. She's not warm but she's direct. She tells them: *"This is new. Someone made this tear. The old tears weren't like this."* She has records. She's been tracking a pattern for months. She'll share it if they close the eastern tear.

### Scene 3: Closing the Tear (First Real Challenge)

Idris's method: a stabilization ritual, slow and costly — it will take something from her (a memory, a year, something she holds). She's matter-of-fact about this.

**The choice:** Let Idris do the ritual (works, costs her something, she thanks them without sentiment), or find another way (harder — requires information gathered from the village, more time, more understanding of how the tear works). A third option: do nothing and try to just pass through it (dangerous, possible, loses the relationship with Idris).

*If the ritual is used:* Idris closes the tear. The village stabilizes. She tells them about the Mender trail she's found — a scholar who was here six weeks ago, doing "Veil research." The tears appeared two weeks later.

### Scene 4: Finding the Trail

Following Idris's information leads to the Mender's campsite outside the village — abandoned but intact. Notes, equipment, a map. The companions piece together: someone was deliberately manipulating a Veil-point here. Not for destruction. For study, or repair, or something they believed was repair.

The map shows the next location. There's a tear near it — unstable, but Sarro can bring them through.

**Tone moment:** Before they leave, Liris helps finish the harvest she started that morning, before all of this. Sarro watches from a fence post, not understanding why. The neighbor who called to her that morning is there. Nobody says goodbye exactly. The village doesn't make a fuss.

Liris picks up her bag. Walks to Sarro. *"You're certain you can get us through?"* He says yes. She looks at the tear. *"Then let's go."*

She doesn't look back.

---

## Act 1 Ends

They go through the tear. For a moment — brief, strange, beautiful — they're in the Between. The impossible geometry of the Veil itself. Then: The Reach. Wide open sky. Cattle in the distance. Wind.

**What changed:**
- Player understands the combat system, the dialogue system, the world's rules
- Liris is established: capable, angry, moving toward something
- Sarro is established: competent, understated, genuinely in debt
- The Mender trail is established as the through-line
- Idris is a potential future contact (she knows more; she'll appear again)

**The emotional question going into Act 2:** *Who did this, and why, and will we ever catch them?*
